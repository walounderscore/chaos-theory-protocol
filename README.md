# chaos theory protocol:

self-hosting for artists who love the music most.
<u>Music is emotions stored in code.</u>

What's your goal when your music is shared?
## **connecting. influence. business.**

Depending on your motivation, the route is different.
* **emotions**→connecting. (how do they receive the emotions?)
* **connections**→influence. (where do they send the emotions?)
* **transactions**→business. (why do they keep the emotions?)

They're streamlined. While your priority adapts to your environment, self-hosting is for artists willing to hedge their music against industrial fluctuations.

It's for artists with little to lose from full independence. No interruptions allowed.

The **chaos theory protocol** provides a **plug & play foundation** to build your own decentralised distribution pipeline to each goal using open-source and low-risk infrastructure.

Your music lives on your domain, marketed and maintained by you (as any worker does with their craft and tools), to earn a **reputation worth sharing** and establish **value worth trading for**.

### **this is for you if:**

1. **you never want your music taken down again.**
2. **you want direct streaming data from your listeners.**
3. **you are willing to/already handle your own marketing.**
4. **your music can make you more than streaming revenue.**
5. **creative freedom with your cover artwork is a priority.**
6. **you'll sell licenses to small creators and larger budgets.**
7. **you don't want to keep paying after all the work to release music.**
8. **you own your professional website to embed this tool.**
